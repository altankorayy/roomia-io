User-Uploaded Photos and Images

The core functionality of the Application requires you to upload photographs of interior rooms, exterior spaces, gardens, and other areas you wish to redesign using artificial intelligence. By uploading photos to the Application, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

  • Purpose of Collection: Photos are collected solely to enable the Application's AI-powered redesign functionality. The Service Provider does not use, sell, license, share, or otherwise exploit your uploaded photos for any purpose other than delivering the Application's core service to you.
  • Original and AI-Generated Images: Both your original uploaded photos and the AI-generated redesign results are stored in Google Firebase Storage, which is operated by Google LLC. These images are associated with your anonymous user account and are retained to allow you to access your design history within the Application.
  • Design Metadata: Information related to your design requests — including selected design styles, prompts, timestamps, and processing status — is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of the Firebase platform). This metadata is necessary for the Application to function and to display your design history.
  • No Manual Processing: The Service Provider does not manually review, analyze, or process your uploaded photos. All image processing is performed automatically through third-party AI services as described below.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing and Third-Party AI Services

The Application uses third-party artificial intelligence models to generate redesigned images from your uploaded photos. By using the Application, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

  • AI Service Provider — Replicate, Inc.: Your uploaded photos are transmitted to Replicate, Inc. ("Replicate"), a third-party AI infrastructure provider, for processing by AI models. This transmission is necessary for the Application to generate redesigned images. Replicate processes your images on its servers and returns the AI-generated results to the Application.
  • Temporary Storage by Replicate: Replicate temporarily stores uploaded images and generated outputs on its servers for operational purposes. According to Replicate's data handling practices, these files are automatically deleted approximately one (1) hour after processing. The Service Provider has no control over Replicate's internal data handling beyond this stated policy.
  • AI Model Processing: The AI models used through Replicate may process your images as part of their inference operations. While the Service Provider selects AI models that do not use customer input data for training by default, the Service Provider cannot guarantee the internal data handling practices of every AI model provider. You should be aware that by using AI-powered features, your images are processed by third-party machine learning systems.
  • Replicate's Privacy Policy: Replicate's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy, available at https://replicate.com/privacy. The Service Provider encourages you to review Replicate's privacy policy to understand how your data may be handled during AI processing.

All user data, including uploaded photos, AI-generated images, design metadata, user profiles, and subscription information, is stored and processed within Firebase services. Firebase's handling of data is governed by Google's privacy policies and terms of service. Firebase provides industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and infrastructure security. This data is not automatically or periodically deleted by the Service Provider, as it is necessary to provide you with continued access to your design history and the Application's core functionality.

If you wish to have your data deleted — including all uploaded photos, AI-generated images, design metadata, and any other personal data associated with your account — you may submit a deletion request through the in-app support feature (Settings → Contact Support) or by contacting the Service Provider directly at altankoraybenli@gmail.com. Upon receiving a verified deletion request, the Service Provider will permanently delete all of your data from Firebase Storage and Cloud Firestore within thirty (30) calendar days. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Please note that once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered, and you will lose access to all previously generated designs and design history.
